# Round 4 — Cutaway, breach, and analyze

**Goal:** turn the three non-establishing scenes into readable, art-directed views with their own light.
**Date:** 2026-10-24

## Reference study

- Igloo's detail and portfolio scenes: a single object, a cool studio plate, one accent, thin callouts.
- Architectural section drawings: cut faces with wall thickness, a raised floor, bulkheads, a legible module hierarchy.
- The BULKHEAD brief: the cutaway should be warm and readable; the breach should communicate stakes; ANALYZE should be a pressure-network lens, not a dashboard.

## The nine biggest weaknesses found

1. The cutaway was side-on and flat: a beige tunnel with a dollhouse read.
2. The interior was uniformly orange with no cool contrast and no visible practicals.
3. The section floated above the regolith; the cut edge was a thin orange line.
4. The airlock end had a spiral-staircase-like white mess.
5. The breach was an extreme close-up of a wall with a giant white smoke cloud and floating rails.
6. The breach's hull was gold/brown (the ceramic hull was not visible) and the red light was a full wash.
7. ANALYZE was just the exterior with a data panel; no overlay, no cool mode language.
8. The analyze panel overlapped the air ledger; labels crossed the bright hull.
9. DESIGN was still just labels on the exterior — deferred to Round 5.

## Changes

- Cutaway: moved to a 3/4 camera, added three bulkhead partitions with rounded doorways, a raised grated floor, walkway, cable tray, ladder, lockers, galley, ECLSS tanks and two cyan displays. Replaced the orange wash with four warm practicals, cool screen light and a soft CREW shaft. Added a layered cut face (titanium + warm liner + white ceramic skin) and a dark plinth with support beams.
- Breach: reframed to a medium shot showing the hull, airlock, ground and sky; replaced the cloud with a narrow white-blue jet, 90 small particles, a frost ring and a red pressure contour; added a visible red beacon and kept the hull white.
- ANALYZE: added a cyan wireframe (5 rings + 8 lines), four pressure nodes with halos and a connecting line, a LAB contour ring, a soft scan plane, a cool/desaturated grade, and a right-hand pressure panel with four sparklines and values.
- Fixed panel overlap by moving the data panel to the right; added callout scrims and 11 px labels.
- Verified the cutaway is grounded on a plinth and all four bays read.

## Harsh critique

The cutaway finally read as an architectural section, and ANALYZE became a real mode. But the **cutaway was too dark** in the upper walls and CREW/ECLSS; the practicals lit pools rather than the space. The near cut ring still silhouetted heavily and the end hatch was a black oval. The white ceramic skin was too subtle at the 3/4 angle.

The breach was better in context but the **hull still dominated the frame**, the red was a broad soft pool rather than a rim, and the 1 cm puncture bloomed into a spark. The frost read as a small starburst. Floating rails were removed, but the door panels still read as dark boxes.

ANALYZE's language was the strongest of the three, but the solar array's white frames blew out at the left (later hidden) and the right-hand ground clipped white. The labels were still small.
